What the current language says

The implementation is capable and coherent, but three separate signals all communicate the same fact: colored container, colored inset rail, and group header.

StrongClear ownership. A rounded container makes membership unambiguous.
StrongUseful mechanics. Rename, recolor, collapse, add, drag, ungroup, and close already exist.
FrictionColor dominates content. A 22% fill across every grouped tab becomes the loudest surface in the rail.
FrictionSelection competes with grouping. The selected-tab wash has to sit inside a second colored wash.
GapActions are split. Add is visible; rename, color, ungroup, and close depend on right-click discovery.

Ideas worth combining

Independent of the visual direction
DiscoveryHover overflow

Reveal a small ⋯ action beside + so rename, recolor, ungroup, and close are discoverable without removing the context menu.

FocusIsolate group

A group action temporarily shows only that group, with an “All tabs” breadcrumb to return. No terminal process moves.

AutomationSuggest, never impose

Detect shared repository, host, or command context and offer one-click grouping. Manual groups remain authoritative.

DragPreview the destination

During drag, open a collapsed target after a short hover and show whether the result is reorder, join, or move whole group.
